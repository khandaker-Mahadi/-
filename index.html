My Story

Khandaker Mahadi Class 9 Student, Dhaka
The Beginning

It started with a question — "What if studying could feel different?" In a world where millions of students struggle with organization, motivation, and structure, a young developer from Dhaka decided to build something meaningful.

The Purpose

Students in Bangladesh — and everywhere — deserve a smarter way to learn. Not just another app, but a true companion that understands their journey, respects their time, and helps them grow.

The Creation

StudyFlow was born — a smart study companion and instructor-like assistant that helps students organize their learning journey, manage daily routines, and make studying feel structured, intelligent, and motivating.

"I didn't want to just build an app. I wanted to build something that could change how students experience learning."
